# Contributing to Reel Time

Bug reports, ideas and fixes are welcome.

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue at https://github.com/mebessusn/reel-time-game/issues. Say what you did, what you
  expected, and your game version (shown on the title screen).
- **Code, art or other changes:** open a pull request. Before anything is merged, you agree to the
  [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) by ticking the box in the pull request template. It lets the game keep being
  free here while the maker can also release it in other ways (including paid versions). You keep the copyright to
  your own work.
- **Building and testing:** see [SETUP.md](SETUP.md). Please run `npm test` before opening a pull request.

for any noncommercial purpose.
